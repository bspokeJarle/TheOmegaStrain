# Support And Custom Work

RetroMesh Engine can be downloaded, used, and modified under the MIT License.
It is provided as-is, without warranty or liability.

## Community Use

You are free to experiment with the engine, modify it, and use it in your own
projects within the terms of the license.

Issues, bug reports, and improvement ideas may be considered when time allows,
but there is no guaranteed response time, maintenance commitment, or support
obligation for free use of the engine.

## Consulting Work

Custom engine changes are treated as ordinary consulting work. Examples include:

- Game-specific feature development.
- Porting RetroMesh to another renderer or platform.
- Performance tuning for a specific game or hardware target.
- Custom tooling, editors, importers, or build pipelines.
- Integration with distribution platforms, analytics, multiplayer, or external
  services.

Commercial support or custom modifications should be agreed separately before
work begins.
